Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about this tool.

WEBP is a modern image format developed by Google that offers excellent compression and transparency support. While WEBP is great for websites, not all older apps, design tools, or email clients support it. Converting to JPG makes the image universally compatible while keeping file sizes small.

JPG does not support transparency, so any transparent areas in your WEBP will be automatically filled with a solid white background before conversion. This is the standard behavior for any JPG conversion and ensures the result looks clean and consistent. If you need to keep transparency, convert to PNG instead.

JPG uses lossy compression, so some quality reduction is inherent — but at higher quality settings (90–100%) it is virtually imperceptible. Use the quality slider to balance file size and visual fidelity. If you need zero quality loss, convert to PNG instead.
