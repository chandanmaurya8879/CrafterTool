About CrafterTools

Welcome to CrafterTools, your free online platform for Image and PDF tools. Our goal is to make file editing simple, fast, and accessible for everyone without requiring software installation.

Our Mission

We aim to provide powerful online tools that help users compress, convert, merge, split, resize, edit, and manage images and PDF files quickly and securely.

Why Choose CrafterTools?

⚡ Fast

Process your files in just a few seconds.

🔒 Secure

Your privacy matters. Many tools process files directly in your browser.

💯 Free

Use powerful Image and PDF tools without paying.

📱 Responsive

Works perfectly on desktop, tablet, and mobile devices.

Who We Are

CrafterTools was created by Chandan Maurya, a Computer Science and Engineering student with a passion for web development. The goal is to build a simple, reliable, and user-friendly platform that helps people work with images and PDFs online.

Our Vision

Our vision is to make CrafterTools one of the best free online platforms for image and PDF editing by continuously improving features, performance, security, and user experience.
