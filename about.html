Our Mission

At CrafterTools, our mission is simple: make powerful creative and productivity tools accessible to everyone instantly, freely, and without compromising privacy. We believe that the tools you use every day should not require sign-ups, downloads, or handing over your personal data. Every tool we build runs entirely in your browser, giving you full control over your files, your time, and your digital life.

Who we are

Who We Are CrafterTools was created by Chandan Maurya, a Computer Science and Engineering student with a passion for building simple, fast, and useful web applications. The idea behind CrafterTools is to provide a free, privacy-focused platform where anyone can access powerful online tools without installing software or creating an account. Every tool is designed to be easy to use, fast, and accessible across desktop and mobile devices. As CrafterTools continues to grow, our goal is to add more innovative tools and improve the user experience while keeping the platform free, reliable, and secure for everyone.

Our Vision

We envision a web where powerful creative tools are available to anyone, anywhere, without barriers. A web where privacy is the default, not an afterthought. As browser technology advances with AI models, WebAssembly, WebGPU, and beyond we're committed to pushing what's possible entirely client-side. Our long-term goal is to become the most trusted, most complete suite of browser-based tools on the internet free for everyone, forever.
