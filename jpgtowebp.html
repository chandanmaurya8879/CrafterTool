Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about this tool.

WEBP is a modern image format developed by Google that provides superior compression compared to JPG and PNG. WEBP files are typically 25–40% smaller than JPG at the same visual quality, which makes websites load faster and saves storage space. WEBP is supported by all modern browsers.

Converting JPG to WEBP significantly reduces file size while preserving visual quality, making your website load faster, use less bandwidth, and improve SEO. Smaller images also improve user experience on mobile and slow networks, and save storage space on your hosting.

WEBP is a lossy format (like JPG), so some very minor quality reduction is inherent, but at higher quality settings (90–100%) it is virtually imperceptible to the human eye. In fact, WEBP often looks better than JPG at the same file size. Use the quality slider to balance size and visual fidelity to your needs.
