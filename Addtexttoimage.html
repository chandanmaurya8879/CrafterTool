How to Use

Get started in seconds — it's really that simple.

Upload your image

Click the upload area or drag your image file into the box (max 20MB).

Type your text

Enter any text in the input box, then customize font, size, and color.

Click "Add Text"

Add multiple text layers — each with its own font, size, and color.

Drag to position

Click any text on the canvas and drag it to the perfect spot.

Manage your layers

Select a layer from the list to edit or delete it, or update its style.

Download your image

Click the purple download button to save the final image.
