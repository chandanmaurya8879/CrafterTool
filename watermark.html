How to use Watermark

  • Click the Choose file: Select any image from your computer or phone.
  • Adjust slider: Move the High Quality slider and Select the colour down to a lower percentage to achieve a smaller file size.
  • Compress image:And then enter the watermark what to write in a text then Click Apply watermark to preview the file size conversion on your screen.
  • Download Image: Click Download Image to save it back to your device.
