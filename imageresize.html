How to Use

Get started in seconds — it's really that simple.

Select your image

Click the upload area or drag your image file directly into the box. Supported formats: JPG, PNG, WEBP (max 15MB).

Review the preview

Instantly see the image name, dimensions, file size, and format before resizing.

Set new dimensions

Type width/height manually, use the percentage chips (25–100%), or pick a preset like Instagram Post or Full HD.

Lock aspect ratio (optional)

Toggle the lock button to preserve the original proportions — perfect for avoiding stretching.

Click "Resize Image"

Watch the progress ring animate while your image is resized in real time — fully in your browser.

Compare & download

Use the before/after slider, check size savings and aspect ratio stats, then download your resized image.
