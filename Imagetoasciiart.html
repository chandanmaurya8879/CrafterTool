 Frequently Asked Questions

            
Quick answers to common questions about this tool.


            

                

                    
                    

                        
ASCII art is a graphic design technique that uses printable characters (letters, numbers, and symbols) to create visual images. Each character represents a different brightness level, and when viewed together, they form a recognizable picture — like a photo made entirely of text.

                    

                


                

                    
                    

                        
The tool loads your image into an HTML5 Canvas, samples each pixel, calculates its brightness (luminance), and maps that value to a character in your chosen set. Bright pixels get lighter characters (like `.` or ` `), dark pixels get heavier ones (like `@` or `#`). The result is a grid of characters that visually recreates your image.
