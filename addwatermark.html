Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about this tool.

Text watermarks are simple strings like "© 2025 Your Name" or "DO NOT COPY" that you type directly into the tool. Image watermarks are files you upload — usually your logo, brand mark, or signature stored as a PNG with transparency. Image watermarks look more professional and brand-consistent, while text watermarks are faster to add.

When tiling is enabled, the watermark is repeated across the entire image in a grid pattern. This makes it much harder for someone to crop out the watermark — even if they crop one region, other watermarks remain visible. Tiling is especially useful for stock photos, portfolio previews, and proof sheets.
