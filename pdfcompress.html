How to use image compressor

  • Click the Choose file: Select any pdf from your computer or phone.
  • Adjust slider: Move the Compression Quality slider down to a lower percentage to achieve a smaller file size (e.g., 50% or 60% balances great size reductions with minimal visual changes).
  • Compress image: Click Compress pdf to preview the file size conversion on your screen.
  • Download Image: Click Download pdf to save it back to your device.
