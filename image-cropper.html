How to use image cropper

  • Click the Choose file: Select any image from your computer or phone.
  • Adjust image: Move the Compression Quality down to a lower percentage to achieve a smaller file size.
  • Crop image: Click crop Image to preview the file size conversion on your screen.
  • Download Image: Click Download Image to save it back to your device.
