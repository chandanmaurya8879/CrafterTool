How to Use

Get started in seconds — it's really that simple.

Upload your image

Click the upload area or drag your image file into the box (max 20MB).

Pick a filter preset

Choose from Vintage, Noir, Warm, Cool, Dramatic, Fade and more.

Fine-tune intensity

Use the slider to blend the filter from 0% (original) to 100% (full effect).

Click Apply Filter

Download your image

Click the purple download button to save your filtered image.
