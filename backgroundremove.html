Frequently Asked Questions

Q1. Is this AI Background Remover free?

Yes, CrafterTools AI Background Remover is completely free to use. You can remove backgrounds from unlimited images without any hidden costs or watermarks. We believe in providing accessible, high-quality tools for everyone.

Q2. Which image formats are supported?

We support JPG, JPEG, PNG, and WebP formats. The maximum file size is 25 MB. For best results, use images with clear subject-background separation.

Q3. Does the image quality decrease?

No, our AI preserves the original image quality while removing the background. The output is high-resolution and retains all the details of your subject.

Q4. Is my uploaded image secure?

Absolutely. We use secure API connections and do not store your images on our servers. All processing is done in real-time and your files are automatically deleted after processing.

Q5. Can I use the transparent image commercially?

Yes, you own the rights to your original image. The background-removed result can be used for both personal and commercial purposes, as long as you have the rights to the original image.

Q6. Does it work on mobile devices?

Yes, our tool is fully responsive and works seamlessly on smartphones, tablets, and desktop computers. You can upload images directly from your mobile gallery or camera.
