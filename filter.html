How to use image compressor

  • Click the Choose file: Select any image from your computer or phone.
  • Adjust slider: Move the brightness,contrast,grayscale,sepia,Invert Quality slider down to a lower percentage to achieve a smaller file size.
  • Compress image: Click Apply filter to preview the file size conversion on your screen.
  • Download Image: Click Download Image to save it back to your device.
