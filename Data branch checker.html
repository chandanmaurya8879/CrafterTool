What is a Data Breach Checker?

A Data Breach Checker is a security tool that helps you determine if your email address or personal information has been exposed in a data breach. Data breaches occur when unauthorized individuals gain access to confidential databases containing user information such as emails, passwords, and personal details. This tool queries publicly available breach databases (via the XposedOrNot API) to see if your email appears in any known incidents.

Why check your email?

If your email appears in a breach, cybercriminals may use it for phishing, credential stuffing, or identity theft. Regularly checking your email helps you stay informed and take quick action to secure your accounts. Early detection can prevent further damage.

How to use this tool

  1. Enter your email address in the input field above.
  2. Click Check Breach.
  3. Wait a few seconds while we query the public breach database.
  4. Review the results. If breaches are found, you'll see details such as breach name, date, and exposed data types.
  5. Use the Clear Results button to start over.

What should you do if your email is breached?

  • Change your password immediately on the affected service and any other site where you use the same password.
  • Enable two-factor authentication (2FA) wherever possible.
  • Monitor your accounts for suspicious activity.
  • Be wary of phishing emails that reference the breach.
  • Use a password manager to generate and store unique passwords.
