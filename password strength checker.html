Password Strength Checker helps you evaluate how secure your password is in real time. Simply enter your password to see its strength, including checks for length, uppercase letters, lowercase letters, numbers, and special characters. Everything is processed entirely in your browser, ensuring your password never leaves your device and remains completely private.


How to Use

Using the Password Strength Checker is quick and easy:

  • Type or paste your password into the password field.
  • The tool instantly analyzes your password as you type.
  • View the strength rating, such as Very Weak, Weak, Medium, Strong, or Very Strong.
  • Check which security requirements are met, including password length, uppercase letters, lowercase letters, numbers, and special characters.
  • Improve your password until it reaches a Strong or Very Strong rating.
  • Your password is never uploaded or stored because all processing happens directly in your browser.

Features

  • Instant password strength analysis.
  • Live strength meter with color indicators.
  • Checks password length and character variety.
  • Supports uppercase, lowercase, numbers, and special characters.
  • 100% browser-based processing.
  • No password storage or server upload.
  • Fast, secure, and privacy-friendly.
  • Works on mobile, tablet, and desktop devices.

Why Use This Tool?

A strong password is one of the best ways to protect your online accounts from unauthorized access. This Password Strength Checker helps you identify weak passwords and improve them before using them on important websites. Since all checks are performed locally in your browser, your password remains private and secure at all times.
