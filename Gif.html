Image to GIF Maker

Upload multiple images, choose the animation speed, create an animated GIF, and download it instantly. Everything happens directly in your browser.

Image to GIF Maker lets you combine multiple images into a high-quality animated GIF directly in your browser. Upload your images, arrange them in the desired order, adjust the animation speed, preview the result, and download your GIF instantly. No files are uploaded to any server, ensuring complete privacy.


How to Use

  • Upload two or more images.
  • Arrange the images in your preferred order.
  • Select the animation speed.
  • Click Create GIF.
  • Preview the generated GIF.
  • Click Download GIF to save it.

Features

  • Create GIFs from multiple images.
  • Supports JPG, PNG, and WebP.
  • Adjust animation speed.
  • Preview before downloading.
  • Works completely offline in your browser.
  • No uploads or registration required.
  • Fast, secure, and mobile-friendly.
