What is URL Encoding?

URL encoding (also called percent-encoding) converts characters into a format that can be safely transmitted over the internet. Special characters like spaces, &, ?, =, and non-ASCII characters must be encoded because they have special meanings in URLs or are not allowed.

Each encoded character is replaced by a % followed by two hexadecimal digits. For example, a space becomes %20.

Common examples

CharacterEncoded
Space%20
@%40
&%26
?%3F
=%3D
+%2B
#%23

What is URL Decoding?

URL decoding is the reverse process: it converts percent-encoded strings back into human-readable text. This is useful when you receive an encoded URL and need to understand its parameters or content.

For example, %20 becomes a space, %40 becomes @, and so on. Decoding helps debug APIs, fix broken links, and read query strings.
